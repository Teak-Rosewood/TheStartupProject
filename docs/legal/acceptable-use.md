## You must not
- break any law, or help anyone else to, including laws on privacy, intellectual property, export controls and sanctions;
- upload or share content that is illegal, defamatory, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit involving minors, or that promotes self-harm or violence;
- infringe others' rights, or impersonate a person or organization;
- upload malware, probe or test the security of the Service without written permission, or bypass rate limits, quotas or access controls;
- scrape, resell or sublicense the Service except as your plan allows;
- use the Service to send spam or unsolicited bulk messages;
- overload or disrupt the Service or other users.

## AI features
Do not use AI features to generate content that deceives people about its origin where that is unlawful, to produce malware or weapons instructions, to create non-consensual intimate imagery or deepfakes of real people, to make fully automated decisions about people's rights, or to process data you are not allowed to process. Do not try to extract other users' data or our system prompts. You are responsible for reviewing outputs before relying on them.
