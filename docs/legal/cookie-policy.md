## Our defaults
This kit stores access tokens in memory or browser storage, not in advertising cookies. It ships with **no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies**. If you add any, list them in the table above and in the [sub-processors](subprocessors.md) list, and keep the consent banner opt-in for visitors in the EU/UK and for India under the DPDP Act.
