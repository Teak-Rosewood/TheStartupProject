## Cancelling
You can cancel a subscription any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period.

## Free trial
If you started a {{trial_days}}-day trial and cancel before it ends, you are not charged.

## Refunds
- **Within {{refund_window_days}} days of a first purchase or an annual renewal:** ask for a full refund at {{support_email}} and we will approve it unless we find abuse or a violation of our terms.
- **After that:** subscriptions are non-refundable, except where we owe a refund by law or because of a service failure or billing error on our side.
- **Usage-based or prepaid credits:** unused prepaid credits are refundable within {{refund_window_days}} days of purchase if unused; credits already consumed are not.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges** are refunded in full.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within {{refund_processing_days}} business days of approval. Your bank or card network may take additional time to show it.

## Taxes
Where we have issued a tax invoice, a refund is accompanied by a credit note as required by tax rules.
