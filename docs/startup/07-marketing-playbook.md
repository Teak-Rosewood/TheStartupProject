# 07 · Marketing playbook

> Guidance for founders, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules (especially Indian tax, data-protection and payment rules) change; items marked **(verify)** need a check with a chartered accountant (CA), company secretary (CS) or lawyer before you act.

Short and practical. Revisit monthly. The technical pieces (landing page, SEO, waitlist, events) arrive in phase 6; this page says what to put in them.

## 1. Positioning (one page, write it first)
Fill in each line in one sentence. If you cannot, you do not yet have a product to market.
- **Customer:** who exactly has the problem (role, company size, region)?
- **Problem:** what hurts, how often, what does it cost them?
- **Alternative:** what do they do today (spreadsheet, a competitor, nothing)?
- **Our difference:** the one thing we do better, and the proof.
- **Promise:** a headline a customer would repeat to a colleague.

## 2. Landing page
Above the fold: the promise, one supporting line, one button, one visual of the product. Below: three benefits, proof (numbers, logos, quotes, a short demo), pricing, FAQ, final call to action. One goal per page: join the waitlist or start the trial.
Page speed and accessibility matter for conversion and ranking; the template targets a Lighthouse score above 90.

## 3. SEO basics
- One topic and one primary keyword per page; put it in the title (under about 60 characters), URL, first heading and meta description.
- Write pages that answer the question people search for, with real examples. Skip thin auto-generated pages.
- Internal links between related pages; a sitemap and robots file (generated by the template); structured data for the organization and product.
- Earn links by being useful: free tools, templates, data, and guest posts in communities your customers read.

## 4. Launch channels
| Channel | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Direct outreach | list 20 target customers by name; send a personal message; ask for a 20-minute call. Most early customers come from here. |
| Communities | Hacker News "Show HN", Reddit communities for your niche, Indie Hackers, relevant Slack/Discord/WhatsApp groups. Be useful first; do not spam. |
| Product Hunt | prepare a short demo, tagline and maker comment; line up friends to try the product on the day; schedule a weekday launch. |
| LinkedIn and X | build in public: weekly posts about what you learned. Indian founder and builder communities are active on both. |
| Press and newsletters | tech and startup outlets and newsletters in India and your niche; send a short, factual pitch with a clear angle (numbers, a story, a first). |
| Partnerships | integrations, marketplaces (cloud and SaaS app directories), agencies who serve your customer. |
| Paid | only after organic conversion works; start with a small budget on one channel and track cost per paying customer. |

## 5. Lifecycle email
Trigger emails from events ([metrics](03-metrics.md)); keep them short and useful.
1. **Welcome** (immediately): what to do first, one link.
2. **Activation nudge** (day 1–2 if no first outcome): the shortest path to value.
3. **Tips** (day 5–7): one feature that retained similar users.
4. **Trial ending** (2–3 days before): what they will lose, how to upgrade.
5. **Win-back** (30 days after cancellation): what changed, one offer.
Transactional messages (receipts, security) are separate from marketing; every marketing email needs a working unsubscribe link and a recorded consent.

## 6. App store optimization (mobile)
Title with the main keyword, a clear subtitle, a keyword field (iOS), a first screenshot that states the promise, a short video, honest ratings prompts after a success moment (not at launch), localized listings for your top markets, and the privacy forms completed.

## 7. Measure
Per channel: visitors, signups, activation rate, paying customers, cost per paying customer, payback. Kill channels that do not convert after a fair test; double down on one.

## 8. Rules that protect you
Get consent before marketing email or SMS (India's telecom rules also require registered templates and headers for SMS, known as DLT **(verify)**). Do not buy email lists. Do not make claims you cannot support. Respect platform rules (no fake reviews).
