Frequently asked questions

Can I export my data?

Yes. Open Settings, choose Data export, and we email you a download link within an hour.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes all your projects.

Do you offer refunds?

We refund any annual plan within thirty days of purchase, no questions asked.
