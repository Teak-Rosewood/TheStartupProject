Reset your password

If you forgot your password, you can reset it in under a minute. Reset links expire after thirty minutes for your safety.

Steps

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose Forgot password.
  2. Enter the email address you registered with.
  3. Check your inbox for the one-time link and follow it.

If no email arrives within five minutes, look in your spam folder before trying again.
