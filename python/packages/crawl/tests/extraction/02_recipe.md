# Classic pancakes

Fluffy pancakes for a lazy weekend breakfast. Serves four and takes twenty minutes from start to finish.

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 300 ml milk
- 1 egg

## Method

Whisk the dry ingredients together, then beat in the milk and egg until the batter is just smooth. Rest the batter for five minutes.

Heat a buttered pan over medium heat and pour in a ladle of batter. Flip when bubbles appear on the surface.
