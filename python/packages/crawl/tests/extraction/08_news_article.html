City council approves new bike lanes

The city council voted seven to two on Tuesday to build twelve kilometres of protected bike lanes along the river road.

Construction begins in spring and should finish before the end of next year. The budget of four million dollars comes from the transport fund.

Supporters said the lanes would cut cycling injuries, while opponents worried about lost parking spaces near the market.
